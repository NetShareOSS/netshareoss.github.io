Share files on the same network, without relying on the Internet

Netshare is a free, open-source app for secure local network file sharing. It helps you transfer files between devices on the same network, share files between two computers, or between a computer and a mobile device.

🚀

Local-first & blazing fast

Files move directly between devices on your local network, making Netshare a fast option for transferring files between computers, mobile-computer, mobile-mobile on the same network.

🔒

Privacy by design

Your data stays on your network. No uploads, no tracking, and no external servers involved when you securely share files over your local network.

🌐

Works without internet

Share files without relying on the internet. Netshare works in offline or restricted environments such as offices, labs, factories, and internal networks.

📱

Cross-platform

Share files between two computers or between a computer and a mobile device across Android, iOS, and macOS on the same network.

Large file transfers in action

Built for real-world environments

Designed for LAN-first sharing in secure, offline, and policy-restricted networks.
