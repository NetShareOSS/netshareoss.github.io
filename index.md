# Netshare

> Fast, private file sharing on your local network.

Netshare lets you instantly share files between devices on the same network — no cloud, no account, no internet required.

## See how NetShare works

Watch the short demo to see how to move files between devices on the same network without cloud links, shared-folder setup, or internet dependency.

## Share files on the same network, without relying on the Internet

Netshare is a free, open-source app for secure local network file sharing. It helps you transfer files between devices on the same network, share files between two computers, or between a computer and a mobile device.

### Local-first & blazing fast

Files move directly between devices on your local network, making Netshare a fast option for transferring files between computers, mobile-computer, and mobile-mobile on the same network.

### Privacy by design

Your data stays on your network. No uploads, no tracking, and no external servers involved when you securely share files over your local network.

### Works without internet

Share files without relying on the internet. Netshare works in offline or restricted environments such as offices, labs, factories, and internal networks.

### Cross-platform

Share files between two computers or between a computer and a mobile device across Android, iOS, and macOS on the same network.

## Built for real-world environments

Designed for LAN-first sharing in secure, offline, and policy-restricted networks.

### Internal enterprise

Share files within the company when cloud tools are restricted or blocked.

- No Google Drive or external links required
- Perfect for HR docs, source code, and design files

### Factories & industrial sites

Built for isolated networks where devices may have no Internet access.

- Works on internal networks
- Reliable transfers in offline environments

### Schools, labs & hospitals

Keep sensitive data on-premises while still enabling fast collaboration.

- Designed for LAN-only setups
- Supports privacy-first workflows

### Dev teams & studios

Move large builds and assets quickly between machines on the same LAN.

- Share builds, exports, and media assets
- Avoid cloud upload time and bandwidth costs

## Technology

Built with Flutter for a consistent cross-platform app. Flutter helped the team move quickly across platforms while keeping the UI consistent. It was chosen to enable fast development without maintaining separate native apps.
